In a [previous post](https://www.rockpenguin.com/2010/08/most-awesome-cms-ever/ "Most Awesome CMS, ever!"), I wrote about a CMS called [GPeasy](https://gpeasy.com/).  That post actually seems to still get a lot of hits, which might lead the visitors to wonder why I raved about GPeasy when I am using WP as my CMS/blog platform.  Good question.  At the time I decided to go with a CMS, WP was starting to get really good at being both a CMS and blogging platform and GPeasy was still being baked.  But I might have to revisit GPeasy as it looks like they've added some cool [features](https://gpeasy.com/Fast_Easy_and_Free).Of course, I also stumbled upon [Octopress](http://octopress.org/) the other day, which seems to be an interesting blog platform geared towards hackers with a lot of ways to show code, etc.  I might have to check that out as well.  Options abound!
