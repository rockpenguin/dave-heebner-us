Throughout technology's history there has been winners and almost-rans. Think BetaMax versus VHS.  I don't want to say "losers" in _this_ case because many of those technologies still compete but just do not have the mindshare of their "lesser" competitors.<!--more-->

The example here is PostgreSQL versus MySQL.  I know there are fanboys on each side, but PostgreSQL is arguably the better of the RDBMS out there.  It is often more reliable (when managed by a skilled DBA) and is closer to the SQL standards than MySQL and offers better functionality in terms of multi-tenancy, consistency, and stability.
